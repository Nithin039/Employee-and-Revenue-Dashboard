# Employee and Revenue Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee performance and revenue trends. It provides business insights through KPI tracking, revenue analysis, and comparisons of top- and bottom-performing states, products, and product categories (PCAT) across different years. The dashboard also includes an AI Visuals page to explore data patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- AI Visuals

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Displays important business metrics related to revenue and employee performance.
- Provides a quick overview of overall business performance.

### 2. Employee Performance by Revenue
- Analyzes employee performance based on revenue generated.
- Helps identify high-performing employees and compare revenue contributions.

### 3. Top and Bottom 5 Analysis
- Identifies the top 5 and bottom 5 performers by revenue for each year across:
  - States
  - Products
  - Product Categories (PCAT)
- Enables year-wise comparisons to identify high-performing and underperforming segments.

### 4. AI Visual Dashboard
- Uses Power BI AI visuals to explore revenue data.
- Helps identify patterns, trends, and potential relationships in the data.
- Supports interactive data exploration and business insights.

## Key Learnings
- Creating interactive dashboards using Power BI.
- Developing KPIs and DAX measures.
- Analyzing employee performance and revenue trends.
- Comparing top and bottom performers across multiple dimensions.
- Using AI visuals to enhance data exploration.
- Designing reports to support business decision-making.

## Business Value
This dashboard helps users monitor employee contributions, evaluate revenue performance, identify top-performing and underperforming states and products, and explore trends across different years.
